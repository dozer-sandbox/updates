Dozer Sandbox 0.31.0 — the first public release

Fast, suspendable Linux micro-VMs for coding agents on your Mac: each project in its own small VM that pauses in a millisecond, hibernates to disk and wakes in a third of a second with everything still running.

Highlights

  • Sandboxes that sleep like a laptop — pause, sleep, hibernate and wake; sessions survive every wake and a

restart of the host.

  • Agents, set up and safe — Claude Code, Codex and pi on any base image; their credentials stay on your Mac

behind Dozer's proxy, which is the only way out of a sandbox and asks before it lets anything new through.

  • Your project, live — /workspace is your folder; .dozignore and .dozreadonly keep an agent away from

secrets or stop it changing files.

  • A dashboard (doz ui) — terminals in the browser, restore points, resources, permissions; installable as

an app. doz serve opens it to the other browsers of your home network.

  • Signed and notarised, with a signed update feed and three channels — stable, beta and canary

(updates.mode: off, notify — the default — or auto).

Install


brew install dozer-sandbox/tap/doz
doz onboard

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later. The manual: docs/manual/ in the repository.
